### Executive Summary

The mock Information Security Management System (ISMS) demonstrates implementation of **91 of 93** ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls applicable to the assessed environment. Two controls were determined to be **Not Applicable** due to the operational scope of an isolated Department of Defense contractor environment.

Implementation maturity indicates:

- **87 controls (95.6%)** fully implemented
- **4 controls (4.4%)** partially implemented and tracked for continual improvement
- **0 planned controls** pending implementation
- **2 controls** formally excluded through the Statement of Applicability (SoA)

Areas identified for continued maturity include:

- ICT Supply Chain Risk Management
- Physical Asset Removal Tracking
- Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
- Secure Coding Practices
